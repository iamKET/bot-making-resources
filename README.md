# Bot Making Resources

A massive dump of bot-making resources, including guides from other creators and my own guide. Mainly for JanitorAI and SillyTavern, but the process should translate to most AI chat sites.

## Before You Dive In

- Every creator has their own way of writing bots — there's no "right" or "wrong" approach. You'll find your own style after writing enough bots.
- It took me about 60 bots to lock down my process and template, and I'm still tweaking it depending on what feels right for each new bot.
