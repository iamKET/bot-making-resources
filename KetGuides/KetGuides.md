### So how the fuck do I make a bot?

That might be your question — like "I have all these templates, all these guides, what's next?"

Eh, as I said, each creator has their own way of approaching this. There's no right or wrong way to do it.

To get ready, keep these in mind:
- What's the scenario you're trying to roleplay?
- What type of character are you trying to make?
- World setting? Modern? Historical?

Here, I'll share my approach — my exact workflow when I write a bot. Feel free to try it, or adapt any step to your own way.

---
### Getting Started

Say I have a plot in mind — something vague like "I wanna make a bully," "I wanna be a slave and have a slave trainer secretly in love with me," ~~"I wanna be gangbanged by 5 frat guys"~~... and that's all it takes to get me started.

It doesn't have to be complicated. You don't need the whole thing figured out — just a plot, a vague idea, that's it. Also keep in mind it doesn't have to be set in stone; we can always edit things later.

### Image Selection

Honestly, it's not that deep. I just pick whatever fits the "vibe." A buff guy? An emo? A mafia type covered in tattoos? A knight with scars? A demi-human?

Sometimes I browse Pinterest for reference images or inspiration for the vibe I want — could be scenery, animals, or other characters...

You can also use Gemini to edit your image, or any other app/site you're used to. I like using Gemini for minor edits.

### Writing timeeeeeee

Okay, it's time to write.
I'll grab my personality template (you can use any other template too), then start filling it in.

#### Setting
- Modern / Historical?
- Fantasy / Dystopia / Utopia?
- Notable social structure/lore?

#### Appearance
- Describe the character's appearance based on the image or whatever's in your head — remember, you can always regen the image, pick a different one, or edit it later.
- Any notable scars/tattoos with special meaning?

#### Background
- Childhood?
- Key events in their life?
- How did it shape your character?

#### Personality
- It's your character — I honestly don't think I need to tell you how to write his personality, lol. But here are some questions that might help.
- Introvert or extrovert?
- What are their negative traits?
- What are their positive traits?
- How do they behave in public vs. private?
- Any (weird) beliefs?
- Trauma? Fears? Secrets?
- How do they treat other people?

#### NPCs
- Friends/family...

#### Daily habits, quirks
- Work/study
- Entertainment/hobbies
- Some weird shit (optional)
- Anything else?

#### Kinks
- How I imagine they'd fuck me (yeah, you read that right), or how I'd like them to fuck me
- Orientation? Past sexual experience?

#### Speech
- Overall style — cursing? Accent? Quirks? Pauses? Do they use another language?
- Example speech (when they're angry, when they're teasing, etc.)

#### Rambling/AI usage
- I usually just ramble and type a bunch of stuff into one fat paragraph, then send it to AI to sort into my categories/template, or just to reword my rambling.
- It's completely fine to use AI to assist you.
- I'd avoid throwing a trope at AI and telling it to write the rest for you — it'll spit out generic, tropey, boring stuff (yes, I've tried it).

#### Test your bots
- I test my bots a lot, with empty intros and later on with intro written to see if their's behaviors and dialogues matches what I expected. 
- I sometimes edit my bots 3-10+ times depending on each bot before finally releasing them. 
- For testing, i simply roleplay with my bots using 2-3 different persona to see their's reactions, or just one persona using various scenarios. 
- Sometimes I decided to rewrite the whole personality because it's a completely shit show and AI intepreted my stuff in a way that I didn't expect.
- It takes me 2-3 days of writing and editing to complete a bot. Sometimes 5-7 days. 
- It's completely fine to ended up not liking your bots after, we've all been there, done that.

#### Side note
- AI doesn't handle "negative" wording like "no" / "not" / "doesn't" well.
- It's better to word things using positive reinforcement — like "will," "always," "avoid."
